REIKI HEALING

A space to soften.
A return to you.

Sometimes we spend so much time caring for everyone else that we forget what it feels like to simply receive. Reiki offers a quiet space to pause, breathe and reconnect with yourself.

As a Reiki Master, I work with Reiki healing energy, gentle presence and intuition. My intention is to support you as a whole person—body, mind, heart and spirit—and to hold space for whatever you are carrying that day.

You might come feeling overwhelmed, emotionally tired or disconnected from yourself. You may be moving through grief, change or a difficult season, or simply feel drawn to some time that is just for you. You do not need to arrive with the right words or a clear intention. We can begin exactly where you are.

What can a session support?

My sessions offer space for rest, emotional reflection and spiritual connection. People come seeking calm during stressful times, comfort through grief and change, or a chance to listen inwardly when life feels noisy. If you are living with illness or pain, this can also be time to receive gentle, personal care alongside your usual healthcare.

Healing, to me, is remembering. Remembering your own wisdom, your own worth and the connection that can become harder to hear beneath the demands of everyday life. My role is to walk beside you and hold the space as you find your own way back.

Guided by you, held with care

You are welcome to tell me what you need, what has been happening for you or what you hope to receive. If you are unsure, I will intuitively guide the session, always with your permission and room for your preferences. You do not need to work it all out before you arrive.

What to expect

We begin with a gentle conversation, then you settle comfortably, fully clothed. I may rest my hands lightly on or above your body. Touch is always your choice, and you can ask questions, move, pause or change your mind at any time.

You may notice warmth, tingling, emotions or a deep sense of quiet. You may simply enjoy having time to rest. There is nothing you need to force or achieve. We finish gently, with time to talk about your experience before you return to your day.

There is space here for all of you. Come as you are.

REIKI FROM THE COMFORT OF HOME

Wherever you are.
Space to receive.

You do not have to be in the same room as me to receive a Reiki session. Distance Reiki is part of my practice, offering a way for us to connect while you remain in the comfort of your own home.

I bring the same care, intention and dedicated attention to your distance session as I do when we meet in person. This is time set aside especially for you, wherever you are.

How I offer distance Reiki

We arrange a time together and begin with a phone or video conversation. You can share what you need, how you are feeling or what you would like us to hold in the session. If you are unsure, we can begin gently and allow the session to unfold.

Once you are settled, I create sacred space and offer the distance Reiki practice through focused intention. You can rest, close your eyes if that feels comfortable and simply allow yourself some quiet time. You do not need to concentrate, visualise or try to create a particular experience.

Making space for yourself

Choose somewhere comfortable to lie down or sit, where you can rest safely without interruptions. You might like a blanket, a cushion or some gentle music. We will agree beforehand how we stay in touch and reconnect afterwards.

Some people notice warmth, emotions or a sense of calm; others experience a quiet pause. Your experience belongs to you. At the end, we reconnect so you have time to share anything you would like to talk through and gently return to your day.

Is distance Reiki right for you?

It can be a lovely option if you live further away, find travelling difficult, prefer a hands-off session or feel most at ease in your own surroundings. You are welcome to ask questions beforehand so we can find what feels right for you.

WHAT ARE THE MUNAY KI RITES?

A journey of connection.
At your own pace.

The Munay Ki rites are a heart-led journey of spiritual connection, ceremony and remembering. Drawing on Andean teachings, we make space to connect with ourselves, the natural world and Spirit, and to carry that connection into everyday life.

Explore the symbolic teachings of Serpent, Jaguar, Hummingbird and Condor—renewal, courage, joy and a wider perspective—alongside sacred space, ceremony and personal integration.

Receive all nine rites individually for AUD $450 per person, with appointments paced around your needs. We agree the timing together before beginning.

Or join an intimate group of three: AUD $300 per person for all nine rites, across three gatherings receiving three rites at a time. Groups never exceed three people, preserving space for individual attention.

GRATITUDE & INTENTION

Despacho ceremony.

A despacho is a ceremonial offering associated with Andean traditions, bringing together gratitude, intention and connection with the Earth. The particular form is held according to the training I have received.

My path includes receiving the Munay Karpay initiation in December 2025, as well as training to offer despacho ceremony. If you feel drawn to ceremony, please get in touch to discuss the intention and what an offering may involve.
