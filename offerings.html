WHAT IS REIKI?

Stillness, presence
and gentle care.

Reiki is a Japanese spiritual practice commonly offered through gentle touch or hands held just above the body. In Reiki practice, this is understood as working with universal life energy. You remain fully clothed, with space to rest and notice your own experience.

Each session is shaped around you. You’re welcome to share what you’re experiencing, what you need support with, or what you hope to receive. If you’re unsure, we can begin with a conversation and I’ll intuitively guide the session, with your permission and preferences at the centre.

There is nothing you need to prepare or achieve. This is time to pause, receive and reconnect with yourself.

What to expect

We begin with a gentle conversation before you settle comfortably, fully clothed. Reiki may involve light touch, with your consent, or hands held above the body. You can ask questions, request changes or pause at any time.

You may notice warmth, emotions, relaxation or very little sensation. Every experience is individual; there is no particular feeling you need to have. We finish with time to reflect and gently return to your day.

As a Reiki Master, I hold a quiet, supportive space for you to pause and reconnect. Touch is always with your permission; you can ask questions, change your preferences or pause at any time.

WHAT ARE THE MUNAY KI RITES?

A journey of connection.
At your own pace.

Munay Ki is a contemporary sequence of spiritual rites drawing on Andean traditions. The rites use intention, ceremony and symbolic energetic transmission to explore connection with yourself, the natural world and Spirit. They are a spiritual practice, rather than a medical treatment.

Receive all nine rites individually for AUD $450 per person, with appointments paced around your needs. We agree the timing together before beginning.

Or join an intimate group of three: AUD $300 per person for all nine rites, across three gatherings receiving three rites at a time. Groups never exceed three people, preserving space for individual attention.

Despacho ceremony.

A despacho is a ceremonial offering associated with Andean traditions, bringing together gratitude, intention and connection with the Earth. The particular form is held according to the training I have received.

My path includes receiving the Munay Karpay initiation in December 2025, as well as training to offer despacho ceremony. If you feel drawn to ceremony, please get in touch to discuss the intention and what an offering may involve.
