PENNY KINMAN · FOUNDER OF HEARTARA

My path.
My heart.

Nursing, motherhood, spiritual practice and the unfolding experience of being human.

Where those worlds meet.

I’m Penny, a Registered Nurse, Reiki Master, intuitive healer and Munay Ki practitioner. I’m also a mother and wife. Heartara brings together these parts of my life, with compassion and listening at its centre.

I was born in the UK and moved to Australia at 24, while pregnant with my first child. Motherhood, love, divorce, career changes and personal transformation have all shaped the way I understand healing and connection.

In nursing, I have been beside people through illness, uncertainty, recovery, dying and grief. Those experiences have deepened my respect for the simple act of being present with someone, without needing to have all the answers.

A spiritual path
of my own.

My connection with Spirit began in childhood. I have experienced that connection through dreams, intuition and moments of knowing. These are my personal experiences and spiritual understanding, held alongside a grounded respect for each person’s own beliefs.

My journey has taken me to Peru and through spiritual initiations. In December 2025, I received the Munay Karpay initiation, a meaningful part of my spiritual path. I have also been trained to offer despacho ceremony, bringing gratitude, intention and connection with the Earth into my work.

Munay Karpay: an initiation of the heart

Munay Karpay is a spiritual initiation associated with Andean traditions. Munay is often understood as love, while karpay refers to an initiation or transmission. It is an invitation to deepen a relationship with the heart, with loving intention and with the living world. Its particular form and meaning are held within the teachings through which it is received.

This initiation is part of my own journey, alongside the Munay Ki rites I offer one to one.

Reiki, Munay Ki, meditation, nature, ceremony and oracle cards have become ways of listening more closely. They sit alongside the care and humanity that have always mattered to me.

Why Heartara?

I believe healing is remembering: reconnecting with the wisdom and wholeness already within us. I do not see myself as the source of someone else’s power. My role is to hold space as you find your own connection, at your own pace.

From individual sessions to shared ceremony, and the creation of my Return to Your Heart oracle, this is the thread that runs through Heartara.

With love, Penny
