My approach

I’m Penny Kinman, founder of Heartara and a Reiki Master and Munay Ki practitioner in Killarney, Queensland. Listening and compassion sit at the heart of my work.

I believe we each carry our own wisdom. My role is to hold space as you reconnect with yours—with gentleness, choice and room to simply be.

Ways to connect

My offerings include in-person and distance Reiki, Reiki training and attunements, Munay Ki rites, ceremony and shared gatherings. I have also created the Return to Your Heart oracle deck, with healing intention at its heart.

You are welcome to ask questions and tell me what feels right for you. We can begin exactly where you are.
