THE INVITATION

Allow yourself
to simply receive.

Healing is remembering that beneath every layer of grief, fear and sorrow, your heart has never forgotten how to love.

This shared experience brings together intention, ceremony, guided inward exploration and quiet presence. You are invited to participate in a way that feels right for you; sharing and receiving touch are choices.

THE RECORDED EXPERIENCE

A gathering from the heart.

  1. Welcome

    Arrival and welcome, with Palo Santo included in the recorded welcome.

  2. Cacao & intention

    Cacao and a heart-opening intention, making room to arrive in the circle.

  3. A card & a crystal

    An affirmation card and heart crystal accompany the experience.

  4. The release bowl

    Intention setting and a release bowl create space for what you are ready to meet or let go.

  5. The Soul Journey

    A guided inward journey exploring nature, the elements and connection with Spirit. Forest, mountains, roots, ancestors, guides and power animals form part of the developed imagery. Your own experience may be different; no particular image or feeling is required.

  6. Sharing & witnessing

    Space to share if you wish, and to listen with respect. Quiet presence is welcome too.

  7. The Heart Blessing

    An individual mantra or blessing and a focused hand-on-heart offering, with permission and conscious willingness to receive. “Today, perhaps for the first time in a long time, allow yourself to simply receive.”

    The circle holds a quiet space: “Your quiet presence becomes part of their blessing.”

  8. Fire release

    A fire release brings ceremony to the intentions held through the gathering.

  9. Food & closing

    Food, conversation and a shared closing, with time to return gently to the everyday.

This outline preserves the flow recorded in the master reference. It is not a reconstruction of the full facilitator script. Final timing and practical arrangements are confirmed with Penny.
