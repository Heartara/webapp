Reiki attunements · Your journey with Reiki

If you feel called to deepen your connection with Reiki, I offer attunements for Reiki Level 1, Level 2, Level 3A and Master. Whether you are beginning your own journey or ready to take your next step, there is space to learn, ask questions and grow in your practice.

Attunements are available one to one or in a small group of up to three people, allowing time for personal attention and guidance. Prices vary depending on the Reiki level and whether you choose an individual or small-group experience. Please contact me to discuss the level you are drawn to and the current price.

Every level includes a Heartara Reiki booklet and certificate, along with continued support and guidance as you move forward on your Reiki journey. Our connection does not end with your attunement—you are welcome to reach out as you learn, practise and find your own way.
