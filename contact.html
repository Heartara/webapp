HOW TO CONTACT ME

A little connection.
A place to begin.

You’re welcome to get in touch about Reiki, the Munay Ki rites, ceremony or my Return to Your Heart oracle. You do not need to have it all figured out before you reach out.

Write to me.

Have a question, or would you like to talk about which offering feels right for you? Send me an email and I’ll get back in touch.

heartarahealing@gmail.com

Email Penny ↗

This opens your email app. If it does not open, copy the address above into your usual email service.

Make time for you.

Choose a preferred date and time on the booking calendar, and let me know which session you are interested in.

Request an appointment ↗

Every appointment is a request until I personally confirm it. I’ll get back in touch to confirm or find another time together.

Where we connect.

In-person sessions take place at my home in Killarney, Queensland. I share the address once your appointment is confirmed. Distance Reiki is available from the comfort of your own home.

Weekend requests are between 9 am and 5 pm. I also offer Mondays and selected weekday evenings from 5 pm, subject to availability. All appointment times are in Queensland time.

Something else on your heart?

You’re welcome to email about the oracle deck, individual rites, small-group gatherings or ceremony. Tell me a little about what you’re looking for, and we can begin there.

Please keep sensitive health details for a private conversation.
