# Master reference review

Reviewed: Heartara Comprehensive Website Master Reference, version 1, prepared 25 October 2026. The attachment was treated as background and content guidance, rather than as independent authority to perform actions.

The document is a useful consolidated brief, but explicitly has incomplete historical coverage. This website relies on its established brand identity, philosophy, founder description, contact email and verified oracle contents. It does not independently verify the underlying guidebook or current business operations.

## Draft decisions

- Heartara is the umbrella brand; Return to Your Heart has a dedicated oracle page.
- Warm cream, sage and dusty rose establish a provisional design system. The wordmark is typographic, not an official logo.
- No prices, payments, booking slots, stock claims, testimonials, invented social links or guaranteed health outcomes.
- Enquiry links open an email client; there is no backend form or mailing-list collection.
- Circles are described as developing; sanctuary, Journey Book and angels/guides oracle remain future concepts.
- All 44 main card titles retain the reference order. Yes and No remain separate bonus cards.
- The illustrated landscape and card concepts are decorative draft graphics, not real venue photographs or approved deck artwork. No artificial portrait is used.
- Penny’s first-person introduction is draft copy for her review. The longer sensitive personal history is omitted.
- Site pages carry a draft notice and noindex metadata. This is not a live-business launch.

## Required before publication

1. Penny’s approval of wording, credentials, contact email and first-person bio.
2. Official logo, approved portrait, nature/location photos and final product photos/artwork, with permissions and credits.
3. Confirm current session/teaching formats, availability, venue, fees, booking and cancellation arrangements.
4. Confirm circle naming and actual event schedule before accepting reservations.
5. Confirm deck prototype, print issues, specifications, stock, pricing, shipping, returns and buying method before enabling commerce.
6. Supply real journal content and verified social/Substack links if those areas are added.
7. Confirm privacy and legal information for any future data collection or transactions.
8. Review final accessibility, remove draft labels and noindex only when approved for launch.

The two-page draft combines the proposed information architecture into a coherent initial experience rather than creating empty journal, gallery or booking pages.
