Care for
your information.

This CRM integration is under configuration and testing. A form is only received when the website confirms it has been stored. An error means the request is unsent; your entries remain in the current page for retry. Requests are not confirmed appointments.

What the forms collect

Enquiries and booking requests collect your contact details, service interest, message and any preferred time. Feedback collects a display name, text and publication permission; an email is optional. Please do not enter clinical records or medical histories.

How requests are handled

When connected, the website sends requests to Heartara’s CRM server and stores them in Supabase’s database. Staff access requires an approved account. Owner email alerts are queued after storage and do not include your full message. An email notification failure does not remove the stored request. No marketing permission is inferred from a service enquiry.

Feedback

Feedback is private until Penny reviews it. Only approved feedback with publication permission appears publicly. You can ask Penny to unpublish a review or discuss a concern through the on-site form.

Access, correction or a concern

Make a privacy or correction request, request review removal, or raise a service concern. Penny may need to check your identity discreetly before acting.

Retention and providers

Final hosting location, retention periods, verified email provider and backup arrangements must be confirmed by Heartara before launch. Archiving is not deletion, and a request may need review against retention requirements. This draft does not claim legal compliance.
